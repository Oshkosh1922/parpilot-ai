# Onboarding and Activation Design

## Core rule

Onboarding must feel like a premium restaurant consultant, implementation team, and operating analyst working together—not a generic SaaS questionnaire.

## Aspiring-owner journey

1. **Vision interview** — capture the dream, customer, cuisine, owner role, and desired life outcome.
2. **Market and facility path** — compare physical formats before showing individual listings.
3. **Readiness** — identify capital, experience, team, evidence, and professional-review gaps.
4. **Restaurant Experience Standard** — define what guests should feel and which promises must be protected.
5. **Living blueprint** — generate facility paths, funding room, first 30 days, advisor team, risks, and milestones.
6. **Venture room** — persist documents, decisions, tasks, sources, and evidence until opening.

## Property and food-truck discovery

The production system should combine:

- Census address and geography services
- aggregate demographic and economic data
- Google Places for nearby businesses and competition
- Google Routes for travel time, delivery reach, and access
- commercial listing partnerships or broker-provided feeds
- mobile-kitchen dealers and marketplace feeds
- local government and health-department sources

ParPilot should score possibilities but never present a demo property as a live listing. Human brokers, inspectors, attorneys, accountants, lenders, and licensing authorities remain part of the workflow.

## Existing-restaurant activation

- One-button OAuth where supported
- Supplier email forwarding when APIs do not exist
- Menu and invoice upload
- confidence-based verification queue
- data coverage and freshness indicators
- shadow-mode comparison
- reversible approval policies
- visible audit log

No high-value purchase, public post, pricing change, or schedule change should execute without explicit restaurant policy and reliable data.
