# ParPilot Product Definition

ParPilot is the cloud operating standard for building, opening, running, and growing exceptional restaurants.

## Three continuous experiences

### Venture Concierge

The aspiring-owner experience begins with a natural-language vision interview and builds a living venture room:

- customer promise and concept thesis
- local market and competitor research
- restaurant, second-generation, food-truck, shared-kitchen, and pop-up path comparison
- property and mobile-unit scoring criteria
- unit economics, runway, sources and uses, and downside cases
- lender-ready documentation room
- permit, food-safety, legal, insurance, accounting, broker, and advisor workstreams
- menu architecture, ingredient overlap, equipment, staffing, and opening milestones
- continuous handoff into the live Restaurant Graph after opening

### Zero-Disruption Activation

Existing restaurants begin read-only. ParPilot maps the systems and asks only uncertain, high-value questions.

1. Connect existing tools.
2. Observe the restaurant without changing it.
3. Build and verify the Restaurant Graph.
4. Run recommendations in shadow mode.
5. Activate only approved, bounded workflows.

### Invisible Guest Experience

ParPilot should be felt by guests without requiring a ParPilot-branded app:

- truthful menu availability
- accurate wait and pickup promises
- opt-in hospitality memory
- private in-visit feedback
- proactive service recovery
- capacity-aware promotions that do not overwhelm service

## Product standard

A ParPilot-standard restaurant can prove that it knows true contribution profit, keeps honest guest promises, learns from decisions, protects owner time, and uses technology to create more room for hospitality.
