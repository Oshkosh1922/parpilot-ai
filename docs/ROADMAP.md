# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Executable product narrative

Included in this repository:

- responsive command center
- daily profit actions
- outcome ledger
- LocalPulse campaign workflow
- Restaurant Graph visualization
- Launch Mode
- adapter architecture
- tests and CI

## Phase 1 — Design-partner shadow pilot

Goal: prove one closed-loop workflow for one restaurant.

- Square authorization and sync
- catalog and modifier normalization
- sales forecasting by item and service window
- supplier invoice forwarding
- initial recipe graph with human verification
- manual inventory input
- daily purchase, prep and promotion recommendations
- owner approval and feedback
- predicted versus actual outcome ledger

Success criteria:

- actionable recommendations on at least five days per week
- forecast error measured and improving
- no recommendation violates inventory or capacity guardrails
- documented value materially exceeds subscription price

## Phase 2 — Operational execution

- purchase-order draft generation
- delivery receiving from phone video, barcode and voice
- supplier variance and credit workflow
- labor schedule recommendations
- Meta and Google campaign publishing
- campaign hold and stop rules
- restaurant memory and station QR knowledge

## Phase 3 — Essential utility layer

- multi-location transfers
- cash-protection forecasting
- food-safety incident workflows
- equipment health signals
- owner absence mode
- anonymized peer benchmarks
- restaurant launch-to-operation continuity

## Phase 4 — Network effects

- collective purchasing opportunities
- service-provider marketplace and reliability data
- opt-in emergency inventory network
- local operational benchmarks
- concept replication and new-location modeling

## Avoid until evidence supports it

- automatic high-value purchasing
- broad dynamic pricing
- invasive employee surveillance
- fixed camera requirements
- hardware-heavy restaurant retrofits
- generalized consumer loyalty platform
- supporting every POS at once
